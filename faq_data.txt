## Electrical Technical Work
What does your Electrical Technical Work scope include?|Power and light layout designing, load schedules, MDB, SMDB, and DB assembling and installing, heavy wiring and cable pulling, cable trays, DEWA incomer DB fixing, meter fixing, and all types of DEWA works.
What is the difference between an MDB, SMDB and DB?|The MDB (main distribution board) takes incoming DEWA power. SMDBs (sub-main boards) distribute bulk power to zones or floors. DBs (distribution boards) serve final branch circuits.
What is a load schedule and why do I need one?|A load schedule lists electrical loads on each circuit, phase balancing, and diversity. It is mandatory for DEWA power sanctions and breaker sizing, and we engineer it in-house.
Can you assemble and test distribution boards?|Yes. We custom assemble, wire, label, and continuity-test MDBs, SMDBs, and DBs in full compliance with DEWA and IEC standards.
Do you do power and lighting layout designing?|Yes. Power layout and architectural lighting layout designing are core parts of our electrical technical scope.
Can you install suspended cable trays?|Yes. We supply and install heavy galvanized perforated cable trays, ladder racks, and basket routes for high-ceiling retail, malls, and warehouses.
Do you handle electrical technical works for restaurants and cafes?|Yes. We have wired six Subway restaurants, Bianca (JBR & Box Park), Caribou Cafe (DIFC), Leto Cafe, and Central Kitchens.
Can you wire luxury villas?|Yes. We have completed villas in Palm Jumeirah (Villa No. 7), Emirates Hills, Al Barsha, Hor Al Anz, Satwa, and Al Kawaneej.
Do you handle industrial warehouse electrical works?|Yes. We have energized 10-warehouse complexes in Al Ramool and industrial facilities in Dubai Investments Park (DIP).

Do you install and heat-fuse PPR pipes?|Yes. Precision heat-fusion welding of PN20 green PPR potable water supply pipes is a specialty of our plumbing teams.
Do you install copper water supply pipes?|Yes. We install brazed copper piping manifolds and distribution lines for commercial and luxury residential installations.
What is the difference between HDPE and UPVC drainage?|HDPE is fusion-welded and provides superior chemical and heat resistance for commercial restaurant kitchens. UPVC is durable, solvent-cemented, and ideal for standard sanitary drainage.
What is an automatic building pressure motor panel?|An automatic control panel equipped with VFDs and pressure sensors that controls dual booster pumps, maintaining uniform water pressure throughout a multi-story building or villa.
