QWhat does AbbadiTech's Electrical Technical Work scope encompass?
A

Our Electrical Technical Work includes complete DEWA approvals, power layout designing, light layout designing, electrical load schedule calculations, MDB, SMDB, and DB panel assembling and testing, heavy-duty cable pulling and wiring, cable tray and ladder fabrication, DEWA incomer DB fixing, smart kilowatt-hour meter fixing, and full electrical energization.

QWhat is the difference between MDB, SMDB, and DB panels?
A

The Main Distribution Board (MDB) receives the incoming DEWA high-capacity power feed. Sub-Main Distribution Boards (SMDB) distribute this bulk power to distinct building zones or floors. Distribution Boards (DB) serve the end branch circuits such as specialized machinery, lighting circuits, and power socket outlets.

QWhy is a certified Load Schedule essential for DEWA approvals?
A

A Load Schedule calculates the exact connected load, diversified maximum demand, cable sizing, and circuit breaker ratings required for your premises. DEWA requires an engineered load schedule before issuing power sanctions, connection approvals, or meter releases.

Yes. AbbadiTech designs, assembles, wires, labels, and continuity-tests MDB, SMDB, and DB panels following strict DEWA technical regulations and IEC standards before installation on site.
