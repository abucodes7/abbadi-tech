Plumbing Technical Work FAQs

Expert answers regarding PPR pipe fusion, HDPE drainage, booster pump panels, and luxury bathroom installations in Dubai.

QWhat services are included under AbbadiTech's Plumbing Technical Work?
A

Our Plumbing Technical Work covers PPR hot & cold water supply piping, copper pipe installations, HDPE and UPVC gravity drainage systems, high-pressure (HP) hydraulic systems, water booster and transfer pump assemblies, automatic building pressure motor control panels, water heaters and solar thermal systems, and luxury sanitary fixture installations.

QWhy do you use PN20 PPR piping for domestic water supply lines?
A

PPR (Polypropylene Random Copolymer) PN20 pipes are heat-fusion welded to create leak-free, corrosion-resistant, and chemically inert water lines capable of withstanding the high operating temperatures and water pressure demands typical in Dubai buildings.

QWhat is an Automatic Building Pressure Motor Panel?
A

An Automatic Pressure Motor Panel controls variable frequency drives (VFD) or pressure switches connected to dual booster pumps. It ensures uninterrupted, constant water pressure across all multi-story outlets and villa fixtures while preventing pump motor burn-out.

QWhat is the difference between HDPE and UPVC drainage systems?
A

HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene) is fusion-welded or electrofusion-jointed, offering exceptional thermal resistance and chemical tolerance—making it ideal for commercial kitchens, laboratories, and acoustic drainage. UPVC is solvent-cemented and provides an economical, durable standard for sanitary waste and stormwater.

QCan you install luxury bathroom sanitary fittings and concealed cisterns?
A

Yes. Our master plumbers install high-end sanitary brands (such as Geberit, Grohe, Kohler, Dornbracht, and Hansgrohe) including concealed cistern wall frames, thermostatic rain showers, free-standing bathtubs, sensor faucets, and luxury bidet systems.

QDo you supply and install solar water heaters and central heating systems?
A

Yes. We install both centralized solar thermal water heaters with circulation pumps and localized electric water heaters with all necessary safety pressure relief valves and expansion vessels.

QHave you completed commercial kitchen drainage and grease trap plumbing?
A

Yes. Commercial restaurants require compliant grease interceptors and acoustic high-temp drainage. We have installed complete plumbing systems for premier culinary venues such as Bianca Restaurant, Leto Cafe, and Central Kitchens across Dubai.

QDo you pressure-test all plumbing installations before handover?
A

Yes. Every water supply line is hydrostatically pressure-tested with calibrated pressure gauges at 1.5 times the working pressure for a mandatory holding period to verify zero leakage prior to ceiling and wall closure.
